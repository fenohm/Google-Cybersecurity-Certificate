* *Are there files that can contain PII?*
* *Are there sensitive work files?*
* *Is it safe to store personal files with work files?*

*The USB drive contains a mixture of personal and work-related information, including family photos, an employee hiring letter, and employee shift schedules. Some of these files may contain personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive work information. It is not safe to store personal and work files together because unauthorized individuals could access, copy, or disclose this information.*

| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

* *Could the information be used against other employees?*
* *Could the information be used against relatives?*
* *Could the information provide access to the business?*

*The USB drive could be part of a USB baiting attack designed to trick Jorge or another employee into connecting it to a workstation. The information could also be used for social engineering, identity theft, or extortion by targeting employees, relatives, or customers, while work-related information could help an attacker gain knowledge about the hospital and its employees.*

| **Risk analysis** | Write **3 or 4 sentences** describing technical, operational, or managerial controls that could mitigate these types of attacks: |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

* *What types of malicious software could be hidden on these devices? What could have happened if the device were infected and discovered by another employee?*
* *What sensitive information could a threat actor find on a device like this?*
* *How might that information be used against an individual or an organization?*

*USB devices could contain malware such as ransomware, spyware, or other malicious software that could compromise a workstation and potentially spread to other systems. The hospital should use endpoint security software and restrict or block unauthorized USB devices to reduce this risk. Employees should also be trained not to connect unknown USB devices and follow an incident reporting procedure. Personal and work devices should be kept separate to reduce the exposure of sensitive information.*
